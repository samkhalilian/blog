About
=====

Welcome to my quantitative development notes!

These pages provide learning resources on math, coding, and trading. 
They are based on my experience in academia and industry with the intention to teach my children how to code.

.. seealso::
    
    You can find more details about me at `LinkedIn <https://www.linkedin.com/in/sam-khalilian-453704146>`_.
